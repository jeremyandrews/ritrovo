  - Root cause: Trovato `0149541` (B160) gates `/search` and `/api/v1/search` on a new kernel permission, `search content`. So the pin moves to kernel `main` `e2877ad` (image `nightly-e2877ad`, still 0.107.0) with `scripts/sync-kernel-release.sh --set-nightly e2877ad`, which brings in B158 (revisions, bulk promote and sticky, content moderation, preview), B159 (comment author pages) and B160 (search, pagers).
